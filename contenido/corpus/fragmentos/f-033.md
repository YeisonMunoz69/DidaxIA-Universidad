**Demostración de la regla del cociente.** Sea $F(x) = f(x)/g(x)$. Entonces

$$F'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{F(x+h) - F(x)}{h} = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{\frac{f(x+h)}{g(x+h)} - \frac{f(x)}{g(x)}}{h}$$

$$= \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{g(x)f(x+h) - f(x)g(x+h)}{h} \cdot \frac{1}{g(x)g(x+h)}$$

$$= \lim_{h \to 0} \left[ \frac{g(x)f(x+h) - g(x)f(x) + f(x)g(x) - f(x)g(x+h)}{h} \cdot \frac{1}{g(x)g(x+h)} \right]$$

$$= \lim_{h \to 0} \left[ g(x) \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h} - f(x) \frac{g(x+h) - g(x)}{h} \right] \frac{1}{g(x)g(x+h)}$$

$$= [g(x) f'(x) - f(x) g'(x)] \frac{1}{g(x) g(x)} = \frac{g(x) f'(x) - f(x) g'(x)}{g^2(x)}$$
